Love Island USA Season 8

Meet the Final Four Couples

After weeks of recouplings, Casa Amor, dramatic eliminations, and unforgettable moments, these four couples reached the Season 8 finale. America voted for their favorite couple to win the $100,000 grand prize.

🏆 Season 8 Winners

First Place

Trinity Tatum & Bryce Dettloff

Trinity and Bryce captured America's vote to become the winners of Love Island USA Season 8. Their relationship continued to grow throughout the season and earned them the $100,000 grand prize.

Runner-Ups

Second Place

Aniya Harvey & Carl Schmidt

Aniya and Carl became fan favorites after forming a genuine connection that carried them all the way to the finale.

Third Place

Melanie Moreno & Sincere Rhea

Melanie and Sincere navigated challenges throughout the villa before finishing in third place.

Fourth Place

Kayda Bosse & Zach Georgiou

Kayda and Zach reached the finale after building one of the strongest relationships in the villa.
